# Battle Log

View finished battle records in newest-first order under **Log** on the main screen. On wide screens (840 dp or wider), the list appears on the left and the details for the selected record appear on the right. Initially, the most recent record is selected; if you delete the selected record, selection moves to the newest remaining record. On narrow screens, tapping a card opens the same summary view shown when a script finishes. If **Changes outside scripts** are currently being recorded, an **In progress** entry appears at the top and updates with each change.

Cards use a two-line layout. The first line shows the config name, a small muted account name, and the end date/time on the right. The second line shows the run count, average turns, **Tracked**, and the stop reason. For an outside-scripts card, the second line lists sources in the order they first appeared during that period (such as **Gift box · Missions**); an active period starts with **In progress ·** and truncates long source lists with an ellipsis at the end of the line. Battle cards use a gray background, while outside-scripts cards keep a distinct color. Both card types share the same selection border when selected.

Battle cards recorded via log collection show a key icon to the left of the title, battle cards recorded by screen recognition alone have no icon, and outside-scripts and in-progress cards keep the two-way arrow icon.

A battle record is automatically saved whenever a Battle script finishes with at least one completed run. The battle summary shown when a Weekly mission route finishes is also saved. **Status-check screens shown while paused** and **0-run sessions** are not recorded.

Each record stores the battle config name used at the time, finish time and stop reason, run count and limit, refill count and limit, min/average/max turns, total and average battle duration, CE drops, and wipe/turn-limit retreat counts. The stop reason is stored in the language active when the record was saved.

**Materials** appear in the detail view only when Material tracking was enabled and items were counted; **Bond points** appear only when Bond tracking was enabled.

If [Log Collection](Gains-Tracker-EN.md) was running, **Tracked** appears on the card. Under **Gains (log collection)** in the detail view, you can inspect server-reported items, QP, Master EXP, per-Servant bond gains, fruit and other item consumption (such as Storm Pods and Teapots), and a collapsible per-battle breakdown. Gain/use tiles wrap to fit the screen width, showing icon + short name + count for items and icon + count for currencies. Tapping or long-pressing a tile does not open a popup, while accessibility screen readers receive the name and count. If an icon fails to load, the name is shown instead. Items not yet present in KR data are displayed under their Japanese names until KR data updates. Missing icons are downloaded once on demand from Atlas's public HTTPS server and cached permanently inside the app; subsequent views reuse the saved files, and no account data is uploaded. If any battles could not be captured, a missing-battles notice is shown. Logs without capture data show only the standard summary.

In **Changes outside scripts** details, totals and expanded per-source breakdowns display signed, digit-grouped deltas for currencies such as QP, Saint Quartz, and Friend points. Even if only Friend points change, a new period starts or appends to the in-progress record. Friend Gacha usage and any newly obtained Servants/CEs included in the response appear under **Summon**. Friend points received through other routes, such as support-use rewards, are counted under the source of that response.

Changes caused by non-battle FGA scripts (Friend Gacha, Lottery, Gift Box, Enhancement, Auto Skill Level / Auto Append Skill Level, etc.) are also recorded under **Changes outside scripts**. Newly recorded changes while a non-battle script is running are tagged with the **Script** source, and changes after the script finishes return to their original sources (such as Gift box or Summon). Both are separated within the same period, while older existing records keep their original sources. Starting a Battle script (standard Battle or Weekly mission route) closes any active period, and changes during the battle go only into the battle log. The period also closes at the first login after the game day changes (daily reset at 04:00 KST/JST), and what that login grants, such as Friend Points, goes into the new period. A quest cleared after 04:00 that was started before it is still counted in the earlier day's period, because the forced re-login comes later; logins within the same game day and app restarts keep the period.

Tapping the **filter icon** next to **Delete** at the top opens a dialog to configure Date, Profile, Source, Gained resource, and Records to keep. For **Date**, choose **Today / 7 days / 30 days / All** or pick start and end dates under **Custom range** (inclusive on both ends, based on the device's local date and each record's end time). For **Profile**, choose **All profiles** or a profile name appearing in stored logs (**#account ID** if unnamed). Selecting a profile filters both battle and outside-scripts records to that account; records without an account ID (runs without log collection or older logs lacking profile metadata) appear under **All profiles**, or by choosing **No profile** at the bottom of the list when present. If no records exist for the selected profile (or **No profile**), the view falls back to **All profiles**, while remembering the selection across rotation or reload so the filter reapplies once matching records appear. Profile filtering combines with date and resource filters, and the resource picker list follows the selected date range and profile. For **Gained resource**, choose from the names and icons appearing in those logs You can pick **several** resources: tap a chip to switch it on or off, and **All resources** clears the selection. A record is shown when it gained **any** of the chosen resources, and chosen resources stay visible while you search. A resource that disappears from the list after you change the date, profile or source is dropped from the filter. Friend points appear in the resource list just like QP, and outside-scripts records appear when they contain a gain of that resource. Tap **Apply** to apply the filters. When filters are active, a badge appears on the filter icon alongside a one-line summary above the list (tap it to open the dialog). Resources are joined with " · " and shortened to the first two plus a count beyond three. **Reset** at the bottom of the dialog returns date, profile, source, resources and the search text to their defaults; the change takes effect only after **Apply**, and the button is disabled when everything is already at its default. Records to keep is not reset. Active filters and pending dialog selections survive screen rotation, and an empty-filter notice is shown if no records match.

For **Source**, choose **All sources / Script / Gift box / Shop / Summon / Lottery / Enhancement / AP recovery / Quests played by hand / Missions / Other**. **Script** includes all battle logs as well as changes newly recorded while a non-battle FGA script was running. Outside-scripts records match if any source in the period matches, and their detail view highlights the matching source first while keeping the remaining entries. Source filtering combines with date, profile, and gained resource, and the resource picker list also follows the selected source. The active source filter appears in the summary line above the list, and both pending and applied selections survive rotation and screen restoration. **All sources** removes source filtering, and **Reset** followed by **Apply** clears all filters together.

**By day view**: the **View** section at the top of the filter dialog (the filter icon) switches between **By record** and **By day**. The choice takes effect on **Apply**, **Cancel** keeps the current view, and **Reset** does not change it because the view is not a filter. While By day is on, the summary row above the list shows **By day**. By day groups the records that pass the filters by **game day**, newest day first. The game day changes every day at **04:00**. KR and JP both use a fixed UTC+9 with no daylight saving, so the grouping does not depend on the device time zone; when the device is not on Korea/Japan time, the day header says so. Times on cards and details still use the device time zone. The list has **one row per game day and account**: with two accounts on the same day there are two rows for that date, and each row has two lines: the date, then the account name with its server (KR/JP) and the battle count. Inside a day the rows follow the account order, with the **No profile** row last. The filters run first, so choosing one account in the profile filter leaves only that account's days. On a wide screen the left pane lists these rows and the right pane shows the selected row's **changes** and **scripts run**; on a narrow screen tapping a row opens its detail and Back returns to the list. The detail shows only the selected account's changes and battle records. The changes sit directly on the page without a card background, under a heading with the date and the account name and server (KR/JP), and add the **log-collection gains of the battle records started that day** to the outside changes (**Includes n battle logs** expands the breakdown by source and by battle, and **Hide breakdown by source** collapses it). Material counts read from the screen are not added. Records without an account id get a **No profile** row for each day. A day with only changes or only scripts shows a short line for the missing section. Scripts run are plain list items with thin dividers, not cards; tapping one opens the existing battle detail. Deleting is available only in the **By record** view, and the view choice resets to **By record** when the app is restarted. A record belongs to the game day it **started** on: a battle record's start is its end time minus the total battle time, and an outside record uses the time its period started (the time the period was closed is not used). A battle that ends after 04:00 stays on the day it started and its card still shows the end date and time. A quest entered before 04:00 and cleared after 04:00, before the forced re-login (for example entered at 03:50, cleared at 04:20), belongs to the day it was entered. If you quit the game during a quest and return after 04:00 and choose to resume, the re-login comes first, so the clear rewards count toward the next day. Outside records saved before the game-day rule that span several days cannot be split; they stay whole on their start day and show their end date and time. Because of the retention limit, the oldest day may keep only one kind of record. The filter's **Today / 7 days / 30 days** still use device midnight and can differ from the game day.

Under **Records to keep (per type)**, choose **50 / 100 / 200** (default and maximum **200**). Previously saved values such as **500** or unsupported values are treated as **200**, and when log pruning runs, older records beyond the newest 200 per type are deleted. The same limit applies independently to battle records and outside-scripts records and is saved. Lowering the limit below the current record count prompts for confirmation before permanently deleting the oldest records first; canceling preserves both the limit and existing records. Thereafter, whenever a type exceeds the limit, the oldest entries are pruned automatically. To delete manually, tap **Delete** at the top to enter selection mode, check entries, and tap **Delete selected**. **Delete all** removes every record, including those hidden by filters. Both actions prompt for confirmation, and **Cancel** exits selection mode. In-progress records can also be deleted; subsequent changes start a new period. Deleted records cannot be recovered. Chaldea export is available under **More options → Additional features**.

Recent activity per account can also be viewed by tapping an account card on the main screen and opening **Account dashboard → Recent activity**. Resource tiles share a uniform height, displaying ending holdings and free/paid breakdowns on the second line to the right of the icon.

You can pre-download material, currency, and fruit icons under **More options → Storage → Download log icon data** (separate from Support image extraction). The initial download is about 15 MB and excludes event items. Downloaded files are shared by both Log and Account dashboard. See [Icon Preparation and Progress](Default-Support-Images-EN.md).
