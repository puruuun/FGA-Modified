# 1.8.2 — Support List Filter, Chain Priority & a New Account Dashboard

This is stable release **1.8.2 / 3103**. It updates over 1.8.1 without uninstalling. Includes all changes from `1.8.2-beta.1~20`.

## Changed Features

- **Support list filter** (needs log collection; KR and JP): leaves the supports that do not meet your conditions out of the support list the game receives. Turn it on and off with the **Support filter** button in the play button menu where you pick a battle config; its panel shows the conditions and the result. [Guide](../scripts/Support-List-Filter-EN.md)
  - Conditions: Servants (with the displayed ascension), CEs and max limit break, final ascension, skill and append skill 10, NP level, Class Score +62, Grand Servant, Grand Score +16, bond CE effect, both normal and reward CE slots matching, and friends only.
  - Conditions are copied from a battle config or from a saved profile. Profiles are made and edited under **More options → Additional features → Support list filter**.
  - The result line of the panel shows what the filter did to the last list, for example "Filter result: 6 of 20 shown".
  - When nobody matches, the first person in the list is kept with no servants, so the game screen does not stop responding.
  - Requests the game sends are not changed; only the support list it receives is filtered.
- **Chain priority** (card priority screen): before picking cards, it looks for the chain the hand can make (Mighty, Buster / Arts / Quick chains, or avoiding chains) in the order you set. It is turned on per entry for the Affinity style, and the chain order is changed by long-pressing and dragging. Brave Chains gains **Always**, and the NP card colour is read in the servant details window. An entry with it off behaves as before. [Guide](../scripts/Card-Priority-EN.md)
- **Card priority screen**: the wave or turn entries are listed as a tab row, with the add button after the last tab. The priority tiles are spaced and use three shades. [Guide](../scripts/Card-Priority-EN.md)
- **Account dashboard redesign**: the content is split into the sections **Overview · Servants · Items · Formations · Collection and records · Present box and shop**, chosen from a left menu on wide screens or tabs on narrow ones. [Guide](../scripts/Gains-Tracker-EN.md)
  - The header carries an AP bar, and the Overview gathers currencies, fruit, a Servant ownership summary, storage, recent records and account info (including total and consecutive logins).
  - **In progress** on the Overview shows the weekly mission and event points.
  - Servants are a table with class chips and sort chips (three lines per Servant on narrow screens), including equipped command codes and card enhancement.
  - Items have a name search and class tables of gems, magic gems, secret gems, pieces and monuments (under 10 in red).
  - Formations show one party at a time.
- **Dashboard: quest records and shop**: the **Collection and records** tab shows clear and attempt counts per quest, and the **Present box and shop** tab shows what the account bought in shops with an estimate of prisms spent (≈). The name lists are downloaded the first time the tab opens, for the account's own server only, and never while a script is running. [Guide](../scripts/Gains-Tracker-EN.md)
- **Account state after large responses**: reflecting a large response, such as the shop or the login, into the account state is much faster (the stored content is the same).
- **Log screen**: [Guide](../scripts/Battle-Log-EN.md)
  - The filter dialog chooses between **By record** and **By day**. By day shows one row per game day (changing at 04:00) and account, with that day's sources and the scripts that ran.
  - The filter dialog has a **Reset** button, and you can pick several sources and resources.
  - The outside-script period is closed at the first login after the game day changes.
- **Log: friend points at login in detail**: an expanded **Login** source in the log details shows what was received (Friend, Follow, Recommended support) and the top Servants for Normal, Event and Grand. The game sends only the first place of each category, so the three do not add up to the total. Older records show only the total. [Guide](../scripts/Gains-Tracker-EN.md)
- **Servant and CE pickers**: Servant and CE chips show a face image and sit one per line. Long-press a chip to delete the saved support image; the deleted entry leaves the preferences of every battle config. Images that come with the app cannot be deleted. [Guide](../scripts/Servant-Number-Portraits-EN.md)
- **Support condition items**: the NP level reading, Class Score +62 and Grand Score +16 items appear only after a Servant is chosen under Preferred Servant. Grand Score also needs **Grand Servant** to be on. [Guide](../scripts/Support-Score-62-EN.md)
- **Support score recognition**: Improved recognition of support scores (+62, +16).
- **App screens**: a grey base with navy, gold and red only, and standard Material 3 buttons and selection controls. The button while log collection runs is yellow, and the overlay window corners have the same fixed roundness on every device.
- **Open-source notices**: **More options → Advanced → License** shows the licences and copyright notices of the libraries and data files the app contains. [Guide](../scripts/Installation-EN.md)

## Fixed Issues

- **Built-in CEs on the support screen**: the five tall built-in CEs (Chaldea Teatime, Memories of the Great Library, Chaldea Lunchtime, Mona Lisa, Black Grail) are recognised even when the event bonus label covers part of their band, because their templates are cut to the top 25 rows.
- **Chaldea link conversion**: skills of the `ptOneOther` kind (Himiko & Iyo skill 2, Odysseus) also get the ally target number. [Guide](../scripts/Chaldea-Import-EN.md)
- **Brave Chains and Rearrange Cards in the card priority screen**: for a config saved with fewer entries than waves (mostly configs imported from the original FGA), the screen showed the missing waves as **None** / off and saved them that way ([#724](https://github.com/puruuun/FGA-Modified/issues/724)). It now shows the last value repeated, as the battle does. The battle itself is unchanged.
- **Support image thumbnails**: thumbnails of Servant images saved under an old folder name are found too.

## Known Limitations

- **Support list filter**
  - After turning the filter on or changing its conditions, refresh the support list in the game to see the new filter.
  - KR has no Grand Servants yet, so a Grand condition (Grand Servant, Grand Score, bond CE effect) matches nobody there. The first person is then kept with no servants.
  - On KR the Class Score of Shielder and Ruler is not open in the game yet, so these two classes do not match **Class Score +62**. They will match without an app update once the game opens it.
- **Brave Chains "Always"**: an entry saved with `Always` whose chain priority is off now acts as `With NP` and turns the servant card check on. This applies to configs imported from the original FGA, and to configs where you chose `Always` in the app and then turned chain priority off. Conversely, a config imported from the original FGA with chain priority on now really fights with chain priority.
- **Dashboard shop total**: the prism amounts are an estimate (≈) from today's public prices, and items whose purchase count was ever reset are left out.
- The known limitations of 1.8.1 that still apply remain. [1.8.1 release notes](1.8.1-EN.md)
